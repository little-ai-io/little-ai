How we work

Domain AI succeeds when the people who know the work shape the system. So we do it together.

  1. Discover with experts

    Sit with practitioners to understand the work, the standards and where judgment really lives.

  2. Design for constraints

    Turn rules, compliance and data limits into architecture: what AI drafts, what rules decide, what humans approve.

    Forward deployed engineers build inside your environment and connect to the systems you already run.

  4. Deliver value and iterate

    Measure against real outcomes, expand scope step by step, and hand over a system your team owns.

Principles

How we make AI trustworthy in specialist work

Experts own the decisions

AI does the heavy lifting. Grades, judgments and sign-off stay with qualified people, backed by expert-verified rules.

Constrained, not free-form

Structure comes from standards and templates. Models are confined to the steps where they add value: extraction, rewriting, suggestions.

Traceable by default

Every output carries its sources and reasoning so it can be recomputed, audited and trusted.

Private when it must be

Open-source models on your own hardware or private cloud when data cannot leave your walls.

Geological hazard assessment

AI report generation with a verifiable core

Problem
Hazard-assessment reports must follow national standards, combine owner and field data, and ship with CAD drawings. Manual work is slow and hard to check.
Approach
A fact base shared by text and drawings; templates and rules for fixed content; LLMs only for extraction, rewriting and recommendations. Hazard grades come from expert-verified decision tables, never from a model, and are recomputed independently.
Delivered
A report-generation agent plus a web workbench to manage the report lifecycle, site data, Word documents and DWG drawings with in-browser preview.

AI infrastructure

Multi-cloud ML platform for a computer-vision company

Problem
Training outgrew local machines. Terabyte-scale datasets, scarce GPUs in a single cloud and a mix of server and edge hardware slowed the team.
Approach
One job-submission layer over Kubernetes on two clouds plus on-prem GPUs, with Ray-based data pipelines, distributed training and compilation for edge targets.
Result
Training time cut from days to under 12 hours, thousands of managed jobs within weeks, and engineers moved to multi-node training without managing infrastructure.

FAQ

Common questions

What does Little AI do?

Little AI helps enterprises integrate AI deeply into vertical domains. We work together with the company and its domain experts, and provide consulting, system design, system integration, forward deployed engineering and AI products.

What is forward deployed engineering?

Our engineers embed with your team and domain experts, work against your real data and systems, own delivery through to production, and transfer knowledge so your team can run and extend the system.

Which industries do you work in?

Accounting, legal, geological hazard assessment and AI infrastructure so far. The approach applies to any domain with specialist knowledge, standards and documents.

Can AI run on our own infrastructure?

Yes. When data cannot leave your environment we deploy open-source models locally or in your private cloud, for example a law-firm assistant running on self-hosted models.

How does an engagement start?

Email us. We begin with a short discovery conversation with your team and domain experts, then agree on a focused first deliverable that proves value before scaling.

Do you build products or only consult?

Both. Consulting and engineering engagements are our core work, and we also build vertical AI products with domain experts.

Who is on the team?

Engineers with production experience from Microsoft, Cruise Automation, Walmart and several startups.
